Professional Beach Photoshoot

1-2 Hours Radhanagar / Kalapathar Photography

Overview

Your trip to the Andamans deserves to be captured beautifully. Hire our professional island photographers for a dedicated photoshoot against the stunning backdrop of Havelock’s white sands and turquoise waters. Perfect for honeymooners, pre-wedding shoots, maternity sessions, or just making lasting family memories.

Highlights & Inclusions

  • Scenic Locations: Choose between the famous Radhanagar Beach sunsets or the dramatic black rocks of Kalapathar at sunrise.
  • High-Quality Edits: Get 30-50 professionally color-graded and retouched soft copies.
  • Drone Photography: Optional cinematic drone shots to capture the vastness of the ocean.
  • Candid & Posed: Our photographers will guide you with natural poses and capture candid moments.
  • Quick Delivery: All raw photos transferred instantly, with edited photos sent via Google Drive within 3-5 days.

What to Bring / Wear

  • Flowy dresses or matching outfits
  • Change of clothes (optional)
  • Sunglasses, hats, or personalized props
  • USB Drive to collect raw files
