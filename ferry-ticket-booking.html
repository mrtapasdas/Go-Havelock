Ferry Ticket Booking

Makruzz / Nautika Port Blair - Havelock - Neil Instant Confirmation

Overview

Inter-island travel in the Andamans requires reliable ferry bookings. We provide hassle-free ticketing for premium, high-speed luxury catamarans including Makruzz, Nautika, Green Ocean, and ITT Majestic. Let us handle the queues and schedules so you can focus on enjoying the turquoise waters.

Available Routes & Fleets

  • Popular Routes: Port Blair to Havelock | Havelock to Neil Island | Neil Island to Port Blair.
  • Makruzz: Premium fast catamarans with Deluxe, Premium, and Royal seating classes.
  • Nautika: The newest and fastest fleet with a fully air-conditioned lower and upper deck.
  • Green Ocean: Offers open deck access allowing you to feel the ocean breeze during travel.

Important Guidelines

  • Valid Govt. ID is mandatory
  • Reach the jetty 1 hour prior
  • Baggage limits apply (25kg check-in)
  • Subject to weather conditions
