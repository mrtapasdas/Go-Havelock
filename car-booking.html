Overview

Getting around Havelock Island is easy and comfortable with our dedicated cab services. Whether you need a prompt pickup from the ferry jetty, a round-trip drop to Radhanagar Beach for the sunset, or a full-day vehicle for exploring the island, we provide clean, air-conditioned cars with experienced, polite drivers.

Services Offered

  • Point-to-Point Transfers: Jetty to Resort, or Resort to Scuba Diving centers.
  • Radhanagar Beach Trip: Enjoy the sunset without worrying about finding a return cab in the dark.
  • Kalapathar Beach Drive: A scenic coastal drive perfect for morning photography.
  • Full Day Booking: Keep a cab at your disposal from morning to evening for total flexibility.

Why Choose Us

  • Transparent, fixed pricing
  • Sanitized, well-maintained AC cars
  • Local drivers who double as guides
  • 24/7 support for delays
