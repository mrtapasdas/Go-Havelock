Semi-Submarine Coral Safari

1 Hour Reef Area Family Friendly

Overview

Don't want to dive or swim? You can still witness the mesmerizing marine life of Havelock Island! Step into the air-conditioned, underwater cabin of our Semi-Submarine. Large glass windows provide a panoramic, 180-degree view of colorful coral reefs and exotic schools of fish in their natural habitat, all while you stay completely dry.

Highlights & Inclusions

  • Underwater Cabin: Comfortably sit inside the fully air-conditioned underwater deck.
  • Glass Bottom Views: Huge inclined glass windows offer clear, unobstructed views of the reefs.
  • For Everyone: Extremely safe and perfect for all age groups, including young kids and the elderly.
  • Guided Experience: Live commentary from onboard guides explaining the marine biodiversity.
  • Dry Adventure: Experience scuba-like views with absolutely zero swimming skills required.

Things to Know

  • Reach the jetty 20 minutes prior
  • Comfortable casual clothing
  • No age restrictions
  • Photography is permitted
