Food Delivery to Your Resort

Multi-Cuisine Fast Delivery Fresh Local Catch

Overview

Tired after a long day of scuba diving or beach hopping? Skip the restaurant queues and let the food come to you! We offer a reliable food delivery service across Havelock Island. Order freshly prepared meals from the best local kitchens directly to your resort room or beach cabana.

What's on the Menu?

  • Andaman Seafood Special: Freshly caught Crabs, Lobsters, Tiger Prawns, and Grilled Snapper.
  • North & South Indian: Authentic thalis, biryanis, curries, and freshly baked tandoori breads.
  • Continental & Fast Food: Pastas, wood-fired pizzas, burgers, and healthy salads.
  • Pure Veg Options: Dedicated vegetarian and Jain meal preparations available on request.

How It Works

  • Request the full menu via WhatsApp
  • Place your order & share location
  • Freshly prepared in 30-45 mins
  • Hot delivery straight to your door
