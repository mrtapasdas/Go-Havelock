Snorkeling Trips in Havelock Island

2 Hours Tribe Gate / Elephant Beach Family Friendly

Overview

Experience the magic of the Andaman Sea from the surface. Our guided snorkeling trips take you to the most pristine, shallow reefs around Havelock Island. Float effortlessly above vibrant coral gardens and swim alongside tropical fish, sea turtles, and marine life. Perfect for non-swimmers and families, as our guides provide floating rings and personalized support.

Highlights & Itinerary

  • Boat ride to the designated snorkeling spot (Tribe Gate).
  • Briefing on snorkeling gear usage and safety protocols.
  • Dedicated floating ring support for non-swimmers.
  • Premium sterilized snorkeling mask and fins provided.
  • Up to 45 minutes of in-water exploration.

What to Carry

  • Swimwear/Comfortable shorts
  • Towel & Change of clothes
  • Reef-safe Sunscreen
