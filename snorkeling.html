Snorkeling in Havelock Island

2 Hours Tribe Gate / Elephant Beach Water Adventure

Activity Overview

Discover the magic of the Andaman Sea without needing to dive deep. Our guided snorkeling trips take you to the most vibrant, shallow reefs of Havelock Island. Float effortlessly as you witness schools of tropical fish, colorful corals, and marine life in crystal-clear waters. This activity is incredibly safe and highly recommended for non-swimmers and families.

Highlights & Itinerary

  • Arrival at the beach and briefing by a certified snorkeling guide.
  • Gearing up with sanitized masks, snorkels, and mandatory life jackets.
  • Short boat ride or beach walk to the designated coral reef area.
  • Guided snorkeling session for 30-45 minutes with continuous assistance.
  • Underwater photography available (optional/add-on).

What to Carry

  • Comfortable Swimwear
  • Towel & Change of clothes
  • Reef-safe Sunscreen
  • Sunglasses & Hat
