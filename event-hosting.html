Event Hosting & Beach Weddings

Overview

Turn your dream event into reality on the pristine shores of Havelock. Whether you're envisioning a magical beachside wedding, a motivating corporate retreat, or an unforgettable birthday party, our expert local team handles every detail—from securing the perfect venue to catering, decor, and entertainment.

What We Organize

  • Destination Weddings: Mandap setup on the beach, floral decor, catering, photography, and bridal services.
  • Corporate Retreats: Team-building activities, gala dinners, projector setups, and premium resort block bookings.
  • Private Parties: Anniversaries, birthdays, or bachelor/bachelorette parties with DJs and private cabanas.
  • Guest Logistics: We handle end-to-end guest transfers including ferry tickets and airport pickups.

Our Services Include

  • Venue Selection & Permits
  • Catering & Bar Setup
  • Sound, Lighting & DJs
  • Thematic Decor & Florals
