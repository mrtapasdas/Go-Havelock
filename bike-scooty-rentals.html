Bike & Scooty Rentals

Activa / Bikes Self-Drive Budget Friendly

Overview

The best way to feel the true island vibe is to rent a two-wheeler and ride through Havelock's scenic, winding coastal roads. With dense canopies of tropical trees on one side and the sparkling blue ocean on the other, a self-drive scooty or bike gives you the ultimate freedom to explore hidden beaches and local cafes at your own pace.

Why Rent With Us?

  • Well-Maintained Fleet: Choose from a reliable fleet of Honda Activas, Fascino, or geared bikes (Bullet, FZ).
  • Affordable Daily Rates: Skip expensive cabs if you prefer traveling light and cheap.
  • Free Delivery/Pickup: We can deliver the two-wheeler directly to your resort or the main jetty.
  • Safety First: ISI-marked helmets are provided complimentary with every rental.

Rental Requirements

  • Valid Indian Driving License
  • Original ID proof (Aadhar/Passport)
  • Fuel is not included in the rate
  • Helmets are mandatory by law
