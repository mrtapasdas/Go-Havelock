Deep Sea Boat Fishing

4-6 Hours Deep Sea Adventure

Overview

The Andaman Sea is renowned globally for its rich marine life and big game fishing. Charter a fully-equipped fishing boat and join our expert local anglers for a thrilling day out on the water. Whether you are a seasoned pro or a first-timer, this is your chance to catch GT (Giant Trevally), Tuna, Barracuda, and Snappers.

Highlights & Itinerary

  • Early morning departure from Havelock jetty for the best catch rates.
  • Cruise to exclusive deep-sea drop-offs and underwater seamounts.
  • All premium fishing tackle, rods, reels, and bait provided onboard.
  • Techniques include Popping, Jigging, and Trolling depending on target species.
  • Refreshments and packed snacks provided during the trip.

What to Carry

  • Light, breathable clothing & hat
  • High SPF Sunscreen
  • Motion sickness pills (if needed)
  • Camera to document your catch
