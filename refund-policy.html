Refund & Cancellation Policy

Transparent, fair, and hassle-free procedures.


At Go Havelock, we strive to provide the best experiences. However, we understand that plans can change. This policy outlines the conditions under which refunds and cancellations are processed for our activities, tours, and services.

1. Standard Cancellation Timeframes

  • More than 48 hours before the activity: 100% refund (minus a nominal 5% payment gateway/processing fee).
  • 24 to 48 hours before the activity: 50% refund of the total booking amount.
  • Less than 24 hours / No-show: No refund will be issued.

2. Weather & Administrative Cancellations

Water sports and boating in the Andaman Islands are strictly regulated by the administration and are highly dependent on weather and sea conditions.

  • If an activity is cancelled by Go Havelock or local authorities due to bad weather, high winds, or administrative orders, you will be offered an alternative date/time.
  • If rescheduling is not possible due to your travel itinerary, a 100% full refund will be initiated immediately.

3. Medical Unfitness (Specifically for Scuba Diving)

Safety is our paramount concern. Before participating in Scuba Diving or Sea Walk, guests must fill out a medical questionnaire. If our certified instructors determine that a guest is medically unfit to dive on the day of the activity, a full refund (excluding transport costs, if any) will be provided. However, if a guest decides to back out of a dive due to panic or fear after the briefing or entering the water, no refund will be provided.

4. Ferry Tickets & Third-Party Bookings

For private ferry tickets (Makruzz, Nautika, Green Ocean) and resort bookings arranged through us, the cancellation policy of the respective third-party vendor will apply. Our service fee for arranging these tickets is non-refundable.

5. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds will be processed back to the original method of payment (Credit Card, UPI, Bank Transfer). Please allow 5 to 7 business days for the credited amount to reflect in your account, depending on your bank's processing time.
